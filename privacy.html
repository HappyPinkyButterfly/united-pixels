Privacy Policy Wonders Studio · United Pixels

Last updated: 17 November 2026

What this covers

This policy covers Wonders Studio, the private tool the creator of United Pixels uses to publish their own videos, and its two connected apps: "Jon Wonders Studio" (Google / YouTube Data API) and "Jon Wonders Poster" (Meta / Facebook and Instagram Graph API).

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. The tool runs on the owner's own computer and signs in only to the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.

What data it uses

  • The owner's own accounts: it uploads the owner's own videos with their titles and descriptions, and reads the public numbers of those videos (views, likes, followers, watch time) to choose what to post next.
  • Sign-in keys: the access tokens Google and Meta give after the owner signs in are kept only on the owner's computer (as private settings of the owner's Windows account). They are never sent anywhere except back to Google and Meta to make the uploads.
  • Other people: the tool collects no data about viewers or anyone else. It does not read messages, comments or personal profiles of other people, and it does not use cookies, tracking or advertising.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. The only services involved are Google (YouTube) and Meta (Facebook, Instagram), and only for the owner's own accounts.

Google user data

Wonders Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses the YouTube scopes only to upload the owner's videos, set their details and read the owner's channel statistics.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Google: Google Account - Security - Third-party apps with account access - "Jon Wonders Studio" - Remove access.
  • Meta: Facebook Settings - Business integrations (or Apps and websites) - "Jon Wonders Poster" - Remove.
  • Removing access makes every stored key useless at once. The owner can also delete the keys from their computer at any time; the tool keeps no other copy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to @united_pixels on Instagram.
