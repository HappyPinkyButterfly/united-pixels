United Pixels Life is a video game.

Everyday moments - dating, work, home life - played as 8-bit games. Every pixel and every sound is made by code. A new level every day.

Wonders Studio

United Pixels videos are made and published with Wonders Studio, a private tool that runs on the creator's own computer. It uploads the creator's own videos to the creator's own YouTube channel and Facebook Page (through the apps "Jon Wonders Studio" on Google and "Jon Wonders Poster" on Meta). It is not offered to anyone else.
